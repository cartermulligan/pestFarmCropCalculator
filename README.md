General spreadsheet to calculate pest drops against farming fortune and crop fortune. First page has general information and a space for you to input your current player fortune,
the second sheet on the left has that updated information for how much money you would make per pest on average per insta-sell. Obviously bazaar rates change over time, but this was
just for the sake of feasibility. I think it came out pretty well. The last page just has sources and a data model to see what the comparison is per pest. Yes, some of the data points
overlap, that's because they are so similar in drop rate. That is because of their crop scale number.
